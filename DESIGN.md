# Design

## Theme
Light mode primary, with oatmeal background for comfort.

## Colors
- **Background**: `#FDF9F3` (Crema de Avena)
- **Surface**: `#FFFFFF` (White)
- **Ink (Text)**: `#1C365C` (Navy)
- **Primary (CTA)**: `#5A9BD4` (Sky Blue)
- **Success**: `#A3C9A8` (Moss Green)
- **Accent**: `#E6CBB8` (Terracotta)

## Typography
- **Headlines**: Sans-serif, tracking-tighter, bold.
- **Body**: Sans-serif, leading-relaxed, max-width 65ch.

## Layout
- **Hero**: Clean, asymmetric or split layout.
- **Grids**: Bento-style or asymmetric cards for benefits.
- **Spacing**: Generous white space (py-24 to py-32).

## Components
- **Buttons**: Rounded-xl, tactile scale on press.
- **Inputs**: Clean, border-none bg-white/10 on hero, or soft-border elsewhere.
- **Cards**: Subtle shadow or hairlines, radius-2xl.

## Motion
- **Dials**: DESIGN_VARIANCE: 7, MOTION_INTENSITY: 6, VISUAL_DENSITY: 3
- **Feel**: iOS-like ease-out transitions, spring physics for tactile elements.
