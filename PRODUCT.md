# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Patients seeking medical appointments and healthcare professionals (Doctors, Clinics) offering their services.

## Product Purpose
Connect patients with the best medical specialists in their area through a reliable, easy-to-use booking platform.

## Brand Personality
Professional, warm, reliable, and clean. Inspired by Apple's minimalist and premium aesthetic.

## Anti-references
Generic WordPress templates, cheap stock imagery, dark mesh gradients, and aggressive/cluttered UI.

## Design Principles
- **Trust-first**: Authority and confidence through clean typography and stable layouts.
- **The Human Touch**: Warmth through oatmeal-toned backgrounds and soft accent colors.
- **Effortless Flow**: Minimalist interactions that guide the user to their goal without friction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA compliance. Support for reduced motion and high-contrast readability.
